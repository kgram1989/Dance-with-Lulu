How to dance along

  • Watch a move once, then press play again and copy Lulu, like a mirror.
  • The orange footprints show where Lulu's foot goes next.
  • Dancers count to 8. Lulu counts 5, 6, 7, 8 first so you can get ready.
  • Start on Slow. Go faster when the move feels easy.

Dance safely

  • Clear some space so you can stretch your arms out wide.
  • Dance in bare feet or sneakers. Socks can be slippery!
  • Ask a grown-up to be nearby, and drink some water after.
  • If anything hurts, stop and tell a grown-up.
